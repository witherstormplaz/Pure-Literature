## TROUBLESHOOTING

### "I'm falling behind on the migration"
→ It's okay. Just do 3 poems per day instead of 4. You'll finish by Feb 25 instead of Feb 21. Still on track!

### "The templates are too detailed"
→ Fill in what you can. Leave gaps and come back. Better to have something than nothing.

### "I don't understand a poem"
→ Read it 3-4 times. Look up unfamiliar words. Check online analysis for context (then write your own). Ask teacher.

### "Othello is taking too long"
→ It's dense Shakespeare - it SHOULD take time. Focus on understanding, not speed. One act per week is fine.

### "I'm tired and want to skip"
→ Do just 1 hour instead of 3. Or do lighter work (review instead of new content). But don't skip entirely - consistency matters more than perfection.

### "I'm overwhelmed"
→ Read the System Guide section on "When You Feel Overwhelmed". Focus on ONE note at a time. Progress tracker shows you're moving forward.
